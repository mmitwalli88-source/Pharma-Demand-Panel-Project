SERVICE & STOCK

High fill rate, uneven shortages

Fill rate is 99.18% of requested packs because most months are served in full; 322 product-region-months are not. North stocks out in 9.4% of months and West in 3.9%: volume-weighted service and shortage frequency answer different questions, and both stay visible. Closing stock in April 2026 is EGP 939k, about 15.5 days of demand.

Stockout share by criticality class

Internal service priority, a product attribute covering 25 products. High-criticality products stock out most, but at this sample size the gap is suggestive only (uncorrected p = 0.024).

Inventory position at April 2026

A stock is a snapshot: these figures are the closing position of one month, never added across months. Summing the carrying value over all 40 months would give EGP 32.9 million of "stock" that never existed at once. Cover is closing packs divided by that month's daily demand.

The through-line. The service problem is narrow, not general. A few regions, the promoted months and a short list of series account for the shortages, while the stock carried at month end is modest. That points to targeted forecast and buffer review, not a larger inventory everywhere.
