How much data would confirm a regional shortage gap? study

Share of 500 simulated datasets in which a five-region test flagged one region running above the others on stockout frequency (80% is the usual target). Rows are product-region-months per region; the file has 1,000.

What this file cannot show

  • Whether a new ordering policy lowers stockouts or cost. That needs a sequential simulation of the policy.
  • Forecast accuracy more than one month ahead from a fixed origin. The experiment is one-step-ahead.
  • Anything about real pharmaceutical demand, patient safety or clinical need. Every record is synthetic, and Benford's law on revenue rejects organic amounts (p < 0.0001).
  • Net enterprise profit. Staffing, transport, financing and tax are not modelled.
  • Why stock expired. Batch, shelf-life and recall data are absent.
  • Backorders. Unserved demand is lost in the model, not carried forward.
