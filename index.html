OVERVIEW · FOR THE DEMAND PLANNING DIRECTOR

Review forecasts before you raise stock

Service looks healthy at 99.18% of requested packs, yet 6.44% of product-region-months run out of stock, and 97.6% of lost packs fall in months the three-month average under-forecast. 47% of lost revenue sits in just 12 of 125 series, so that is where the review starts.

Requested demand against the baseline forecast

Monthly totals across all products and regions. The baseline is the file's own baseline_forecast_units, the rounded mean of the previous three months, so it trails every turn in demand. Hover for the month; the gap between the lines is the forecast error planners absorb as stock.

Lost revenue by region

North and Central stock out most often (9.4% of months in the worst region against 3.9% in West). West is the largest region by volume and the best served, so volume is not what drives shortage. real
